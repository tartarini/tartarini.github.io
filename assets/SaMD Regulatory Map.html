What the IEC 62304 safety class changes

The software safety class (4.3) is set from the worst harm a software failure could contribute to after external risk controls: A no injury, B non-serious injury, C death or serious injury. It decides how much of the software lifecycle file you must produce.

Notes and limits

  • Scope is standalone software (SaMD / MDSW). Software inside hardware also brings IEC 60601-1 (clause 14, PEMS) and the device's own standards. IVD software follows IVDR 2017/746, with Annex VIII rules and performance evaluation in place of clinical evaluation.
  • GB: UKCA and, under current transitional arrangements, CE marking are both accepted; Northern Ireland follows EU MDR. Check MHRA guidance for the latest dates before planning a submission.
  • US: the QMSR (21 CFR 820, effective 2 February 2026) incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference, so one QMS can serve all three markets with FDA-specific additions (complaint, UDI and records requirements).
  • Check the edition in force before citing a standard: harmonised, designated and FDA-recognised versions differ in timing, and IEC 62304 and IEC 81001-5-1 are under revision.
  • This is an orientation map for planning, not legal advice. Your notified body, approved body or FDA review division has the final say on what evidence is sufficient.
